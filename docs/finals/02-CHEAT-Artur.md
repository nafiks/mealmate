## MY ANSWERS TO THE COMMISSION

**"How do you know the problem is real? Where are your data?"**
"Three sources. UNEP Food Waste Index 2021 — 76 kg per person per year in Kazakhstan.
A 2021 study of 244 students across five Almaty universities linking food security to
well-being. And our own user tests: three external testers, all completed the core
task, and their feedback became concrete fixes."

**"What did YOU personally do?"**
"I owned the problem: research, evidence, feature priorities and acceptances. I wrote
the problem statement and chose what goes into each release. Example: after our UX
tests I pushed three fixes — clearer ingredient selection, an explicit finish state,
visible back navigation. I also kept the scope honest: we cut features instead of
claiming unfinished ones."

**"How is MealMate different from Supercook or Yummly?"**
"Three ways. First, local cuisine: Kazakh dishes like beshbarmak, kuurdak, baursaki
are first-class — global services barely cover them. Second, the honest two-list
result: 'cook right now' vs 'almost there' with the exact missing items. Third,
language: full Kazakh and Russian interface, not just English."

**"What is the business side?"**
"Today — a student project deployed free on Render. The pattern that can earn: a
delivery integrator — 'you are 2 ingredients away from these dishes' — and university
partnerships for the dorm market. But I will be honest: monetization is not built yet."
