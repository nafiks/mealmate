# DEMO SCRIPT — 2 minutes, live on stage

> The site must already be open BEFORE the commission arrives
> (https://mealmate-29tt.onrender.com — bookmark it, log out first for a clean look).
> Backup: `start-site.bat` (local copy) and `MealMate-promo.mp4` if no internet.

## Flow (click by click)

### 0:00 — Home (already open)
- Point at the screen: "This is MealMate — live, deployed, not a mock-up."
- Say: "Everything you will see is real data from our database."

### 0:10 — Kitchen: the core moment
1. Click **Start cooking** (header) → picker opens.
2. Click **Potato**, **Onion**, **Eggs** (three clicks — the match updates LIVE).
3. Say while clicking: "I tick what I have — the app matches recipes instantly.
   Salt and oil are already counted — they are staples."
4. Point: "**Crispy Fried Potatoes** — cook right now, 2 of 2 ingredients" and
   "Almost there — these need just one or two items" (point at red chips: +Bread, +Butter).

### 0:40 — Recipe page
1. Click **Open recipe** on the match.
2. Point: "Detailed steps in three languages, KBJU per serving, and every
   ingredient marked — green tick = have it, red plus = buy it."
3. Scroll slowly through 2–3 steps.

### 1:00 — Language switch
1. Click **ҚАЗ** in the header — the whole page, including cooking steps, flips to Kazakh.
2. Say: "One click — full localization. Our users think in Kazakh, Russian or English."
3. Switch back to RU or EN.

### 1:20 — Fridge Scanner (our newest feature)
1. Back on /kitchen → click the dark **Scan products** banner.
2. Say: "We are prototyping a camera-based fridge scanner — the camera and UI are
   real, the recognition is currently simulated on the client. It is our next step."
3. Be HONEST: simulated means simulated. The commission rewards honesty.

### 1:40 — Community + close
1. Click **Community** → "Cooked dishes are shared here with the whole university."
2. Close on: "All of this runs on a free deployment right now — the link is on our
   poster. Source code is on GitHub. Thank you — any questions?"

## IF SOMETHING BREAKS

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| Render is down / cold start hangs | Open the backup tab: double-click `start-site.bat` → localhost:3001 (same data, local database) |
| No internet at all | Play `docs/MealMate-promo.mp4` (60 s product video) + narrate the poster |
| Slow first load | Render free tier wakes in ~50 s — that is why you open it BEFORE the commission arrives |
| Camera blocked in scanner | Say: "Laptop camera permission — on a phone it works; the pipeline is the same" and close the modal |
